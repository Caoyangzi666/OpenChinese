# OpenChinese v0.1.5-alpha

OpenChinese is an offline Android EPUB reader for Chinese learners, with word-based adaptive HSK pinyin, an included Chinese–English dictionary and personal flashcards. Requires Android 8.0+ and an up-to-date Android System WebView.

## Reading and HSK improvements

- HSK filtering applies to complete words. Words below the selected threshold hide pinyin; words at that level or above, and genuine non-HSK words, retain their available reading. Raising the level can only remove annotations, never change token boundaries or readings.
- Deterministic word-frequency segmentation replaces greedy longest matching. Tested cases include 看 / 中文 / 书 and 不 / 知道. The algorithm remains lightweight and cannot resolve every ambiguous sentence.
- Dictionary import preserves all pronunciation/sense pairs. 姆 defaults to mǔ while its restricted m reading remains available. Ordinary lexical readings replace several inappropriate surname or rare-variant defaults.
- The word sheet lets you select an alternative pronunciation and its matching meaning before saving a flashcard. Existing cards are not silently rewritten.
- Ordinals such as 第一 and Chinese number forms get explicit estimated classifications. Estimates are labelled, not presented as official whole-word HSK entries. Unknown compounds and names are not classified by arbitrary component-character levels.
- Words spanning EPUB inline emphasis remain whole-word lookups; explicit line breaks remain boundaries. A word stays selectable for a flashcard when its pinyin is hidden.

## Preserved features

Swipe and side-tap page turns, reading-position restore, configurable hold duration (0.4 seconds by default), dark mode, flashcard notes/review/JSON backup, and book deletion with cards retained. Runtime reading remains offline, without an account, ads, analytics or Android INTERNET permission.

## Install or update

Download **OpenChinese-v0.1.5-alpha.apk** below and open it on Android. Install over the existing official alpha app without uninstalling or clearing storage to retain your private books and cards. Export a flashcard backup first. The APK uses the existing official alpha signing key; independently signed community builds cannot replace it in place.

The source ZIP is the complete corresponding source, including the offline dictionary, HSK data, tests, documentation and build tools. APK and source SHA-256 checksum files are included.

## Verification and remaining limits

Nineteen engine regression groups pass, including checks over all 10,896 HSK written forms and regressions for the reported chapter. Five desktop browser suites pass: interface, native-bridge simulation, gestures, preferences and word reading. APK ZIP integrity, alignment, source-asset equality and v2/v3 signatures were verified.

**This version still needs testing on a physical Android device; no device or emulator test is claimed.** Ambiguous segmentation, names and polyphonic readings can still need correction. Follow TEST-ON-ANDROID.txt and report the exact sentence, selected level and expected reading.

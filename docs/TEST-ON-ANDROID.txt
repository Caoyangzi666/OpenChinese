OpenChinese 0.1.6-alpha — chapter opening and pinyin layout

Install over the existing official app without uninstalling or clearing storage. Export a flashcard backup first.

1. Open the long main section of To Live. Text should appear without waiting for pinyin for the full novel. Back must work while preparing.
2. Open Mockingbird and the first chapter. Page turns automatically move between the bounded sections shown in the chapter label. Test both directions at a section boundary.
3. Leave and reopen a book, including after restarting the app. Check reading position, chapter and selected HSK level.
4. Compare HSK3 and HSK4: words/readings remain the same and higher thresholds only hide annotations. Hold a hidden-pinyin word and save its complete-word flashcard.
5. At 26px and 32px, check 年龄 (nián líng) and 沾边 (zhān biān). A complete syllable must remain on one line; letters must not spill onto the next line. Words can wrap between syllables while staying one selectable word.
6. Check the red publisher 注 markers and original note content are still present.
7. Check dark/light mode, swipe and side-tap navigation, chapter picker, existing flashcards, notes, review history and backup/export.
8. If a chapter cannot be read, the app should show a retry option rather than permanent Loading. Use Back or choose a different chapter.

This build passed desktop browser and APK validation. Desktop timings, including a simulated 4x CPU slowdown, are not guarantees for a physical Android device. The release still needs device confirmation.

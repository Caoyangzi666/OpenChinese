OpenChinese 0.1.5-alpha — word-based HSK reading

1. Export your existing flashcards as a precaution. Install this APK over the current app; do not uninstall or clear app storage.
2. Open your book and the first chapter. At HSK1, 第一 should show dì yī. At HSK2 and above it should hide that pinyin. The estimate is labelled in the dictionary.
3. 姆 should show mǔ. Hold it to open the dictionary: alternative m remains available for expressions that use that reading.
4. Change HSK3 to HSK4, then HSK6 and HSK7–9. Pinyin may disappear, but no extra annotations should appear and words must not split or merge.
5. Hold a word whose pinyin is hidden. Save its flashcard and a note. Verify the complete word, reading, meaning and note in Flashcards after closing and reopening the app.
6. For a polyphonic word, choose an alternative pronunciation in the dictionary sheet and save it. Verify that its matching definition and chosen pronunciation were saved.
7. Check swipe/side-tap page turns, reading-position restore, dark mode and your existing flashcards.

This version passed desktop browser and APK checks. It has not been opened on a physical Android device by the developer assistant. Ambiguous segmentation and readings can still need correction.
